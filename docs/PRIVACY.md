# WiFi Database — Privacy Statement

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

WiFi Database is a community directory of WiFi access point passwords. This
statement describes what the app and its server do — and don't do — with your
data. "We" is the project operator; the service is reachable at
wifidb.clickbeans.com.

## What we store

- **Your account:** your email address and a password hash (we never see or
  store your actual password). Your email is used only to verify your
  account, reset your password, and send essential account notices.
- **Your contributions:** when you share a network, we store the network
  name (SSID), the hardware address (BSSID) when available, the password
  you shared, the location you marked (initially your device's GPS fix at
  share time), and the submission time. Shared passwords are stored
  encrypted on our servers and are shown to other verified users — that is
  the purpose of the app. Please only share networks you are allowed to
  share.
- **Your activity:** votes and reports are tied to your account internally
  to prevent abuse (one vote per user per password). We keep a short-term
  security log — which account viewed which network's passwords, submitted,
  voted, or reported — for abuse investigation, deleted after 90 days.
- **On your device:** your sign-in session tokens are stored encrypted and
  never leave your device except to refresh your session. WiFi scan results
  are processed on your device; we only upload the details of the network
  you choose to share.

## What we don't do

- No third-party analytics, no advertising, no data sale or sharing.
- No background location tracking. Your location is used only while the app
  is open, to find nearby networks and pin your shares.
- Donations happen on GitHub's platform (GitHub Sponsors); we never see
  your payment details.
- We never show your email or identity to other users. Contributions carry
  no public author information.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends the error details (stack trace, phone model,
Android version) to our server so we can fix bugs. It contains no account or
location data.

## Deleting your data

Deleting your account in Settings removes your credentials, email, and
votes. Networks and passwords you shared remain available to the community
without any link to you (anonymized), because other people may rely on them.
